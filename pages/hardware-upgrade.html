GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about computer and laptop hardware upgrades.

Still have questions? Contact us →
Is it better to upgrade my computer or buy a new one?

It depends on your computer's age, condition and hardware. In many cases, upgrading RAM or installing an SSD can noticeably improve performance without replacing the entire device.

Can I upgrade the RAM in my laptop?

Some laptops support RAM upgrades, while others have memory soldered directly to the motherboard. We can check your device specifications and available options.

Will upgrading to an SSD make my computer faster?

Yes, replacing a traditional hard drive with an SSD can significantly improve startup times, application loading and general system responsiveness.

How do I know which components are compatible?

Compatibility depends on your motherboard, processor, available slots, power supply and device specifications. We check these requirements before recommending an upgrade.

Will I lose my files during a hardware upgrade?

Most hardware upgrades do not require deleting your files. However, operations involving storage replacement or system migration can carry risks. We recommend backing up important data before any upgrade.
