GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about hardware upgrades, component compatibility and improving your computer's performance.

Still have questions? Contact us →
What hardware components can you upgrade?

Depending on your computer, we can help upgrade components such as RAM, SSDs, graphics cards and cooling systems. Available options depend on your device's specifications and compatibility.

How do I know which upgrades my computer needs?

It depends on how you use your computer and what performance improvements you need. We can review your current hardware and recommend suitable upgrade options.

How can I check if a component is compatible?

Compatibility depends on factors such as your motherboard, processor, available slots, power supply and device model. We check these specifications before recommending an upgrade.

How long does a hardware upgrade take?

The time depends on the type of component, your computer's configuration and whether additional setup is required. We can provide a more accurate estimate after reviewing your upgrade requirements.

Will upgrading my hardware delete my files?

Most hardware upgrades do not require deleting your files. However, storage replacements or system-related changes can involve data risks. We recommend backing up important files before any hardware work.
