1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how NEXORA uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website or interact with its features. These technologies help support website functionality, maintain security, remember preferences, and improve the overall browsing experience.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy. It describes the types of cookies and similar technologies that may be used, why they are used, and how you can manage your preferences.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit a page. They allow a website to recognize your browser, remember certain preferences, and maintain information between visits or during a browsing session.

Some cookies are temporary and are removed when you close your browser. Others remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them manually.

We may also use similar technologies, including local storage, pixels, and scripts, where appropriate. These technologies can perform functions comparable to cookies.

3. How We Use Cookies

NEXORA may use cookies and similar technologies to ensure that website pages and interactive features function correctly. This may include remembering cookie preferences, supporting form submissions, and maintaining basic website security.

Where enabled and permitted by applicable law, these technologies may also help us understand how visitors interact with our website, identify performance issues, and improve the presentation and usability of our computer repair service information.

4. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies and similar technologies are used when necessary to provide basic website functionality. They may support security features, remember your cookie consent preferences, maintain sessions, and enable important website functions.

These technologies are generally necessary for the website to operate properly and cannot always be disabled through our website's cookie settings. Blocking them through your browser may affect certain website features.

5. Third-Party Technologies

Our website may use third-party services to support specific functionality, such as website hosting, security, communication, or performance monitoring. Depending on the services actually enabled, these providers may use cookies or similar technologies.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies. Where required by applicable law, non-essential third-party technologies will only be activated after you provide the necessary consent.

We recommend reviewing the privacy information of third-party providers whose services are used on this website.

6. Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most modern browsers allow you to block cookies, remove existing cookies, or receive notifications when cookies are being stored.

Where our website provides a cookie consent interface, you can use it to accept or reject non-essential cookies where applicable. Your choices should be respected when the relevant technologies are loaded.

Please remember that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. The available controls depend on the browser and device you use.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes to our website, the technologies we use, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how cookies and similar technologies are used.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how NEXORA uses cookies and similar technologies, you can contact us through the contact form available on our website.
