Privacy Policy

Learn how NEXORA collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our computer repair and technical support services.

On this page

1. Introduction

At NEXORA, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our computer repair and technical support services.

By using our website or submitting information through our contact form, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please do not submit personal information through our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us through our website. This may include your name, email address, selected service, device information, and any additional details included in your message.

When you request technical support or a repair service, we may also collect information about your device, including its manufacturer, model, operating system, technical specifications, and a description of the reported issue.

We may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as your browser type, device type, IP address, approximate location, and general website usage information.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, answer questions, arrange service requests, provide repair estimates, and communicate with you regarding computer repair and technical support.

Device-related information may be used to diagnose technical issues, determine appropriate repair procedures, recommend hardware upgrades, troubleshoot software problems, and assist with data recovery requests.

We may also use collected information to maintain and improve our website, prevent fraudulent activity, resolve technical problems, protect our systems, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website, manage communications, maintain technical systems, or provide services you have requested.

If a repair requires assistance from an external specialist or service provider, relevant information may be shared only when necessary to complete the requested work and where legally permitted.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights, property, or users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

We also aim to handle customer devices and any information accessible during repair procedures responsibly. Access to device data should be limited to what is reasonably necessary to perform the requested service.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers are strongly encouraged to create backups of important files before submitting a device for repair, maintenance, or data recovery.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember user preferences, maintain website security, and improve the browsing experience.

Where required by applicable law, non-essential cookies and similar tracking technologies will only be used after obtaining the necessary consent.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the website.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, request deletion, restrict certain processing activities, or object to the processing of your information.

Where processing is based on your consent, you may also have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

To exercise your privacy rights or ask questions about how your information is handled, please contact us using the details provided on our website.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how we handle your personal information, or would like to submit a privacy-related request, you can contact NEXORA through the contact form available on our website.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, or applicable legal requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised information.
