How do I know if my computer has a virus?

Common signs include unexpected pop-ups, slow performance, suspicious programs, browser redirects and unusual system behavior. A security scan can help identify the cause.

Can you remove viruses without deleting my files?

In many cases, malware can be removed without deleting personal files. However, the safest approach depends on the type and severity of the infection.

Do I need antivirus software?

Reliable security software, regular system updates and safe browsing habits can help reduce the risk of malware infections.

Can malware steal my personal information?

Some types of malware are designed to collect passwords, financial information and other personal data. If you suspect an infection, avoid entering sensitive information until your device has been checked.

How long does virus removal take?

The time depends on the number of threats, system condition and complexity of the infection. We can provide a better estimate after checking your computer.
