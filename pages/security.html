03 — COMMON SECURITY ISSUES

Is Your Computer Under Threat?

Unexpected pop-ups, suspicious activity and unusual system behavior can be signs of malware or other security threats. Recognizing them early can help protect your device and data.

01

Virus Infections

Unexpected system behavior, damaged files and unusual activity that may indicate a computer virus infection.

02

Malware Activity

Suspicious programs running in the background, unusual resource usage and unexpected changes to your system.

03

Constant Pop-Ups

Unwanted advertisements, browser redirects and suspicious notifications appearing while you use your computer.

04

Security Warnings

Repeated security alerts, disabled protection features or suspicious messages asking for sensitive information.

05

Browser Hijacking

Changed homepage or search settings, unfamiliar extensions and redirects to websites you did not choose.

06

Weak System Protection

Outdated security software, missing updates and improperly configured settings that may leave your computer exposed.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about virus removal, malware detection and computer security.

Still have questions? Contact us →
How do I know if my computer has a virus?

Common warning signs include unexpected pop-ups, unusual system behavior, unfamiliar programs and browser redirects. These symptoms do not always mean your computer is infected, so a security check can help identify the cause.

Can you remove viruses and malware from my computer?

We can inspect your system, identify detected threats and remove malicious software where possible. The available solution depends on the type of infection and the condition of your device.

Will removing a virus delete my personal files?

Malware removal does not normally require deleting personal files, but some infections may damage or compromise stored data. We recommend backing up important files whenever possible before security work begins.

Do I need antivirus software after malware removal?

Yes, using reliable and up-to-date security software is an important part of protecting your computer. We can help review your existing protection and recommend suitable security options.

How can I prevent future virus infections?

Keep your operating system and applications updated, use trusted security software, avoid suspicious links and downloads, and be careful with unexpected email attachments. Regular backups also help protect your important data.
