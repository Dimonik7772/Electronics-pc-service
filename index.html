NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our computer repair services? Find answers to common questions and learn how we can help you get your technology back on track.

What types of computers do you repair?

We provide repair services for desktop computers and laptops. Our services include hardware troubleshooting, component replacement, software issues and system maintenance.

How long does a computer repair take?

Repair time depends on the type of problem and the availability of replacement parts. Simple repairs may take less time, while complex hardware issues can require additional diagnostics.

Do you offer free diagnostics?

Contact us to discuss diagnostic options and any associated costs before proceeding with your repair.

Can you upgrade my computer's performance?

Yes! We can help improve your computer's performance with RAM upgrades, SSD installation and other hardware improvements.

Can you recover lost or deleted data?

We offer data recovery services where technically possible. Recovery success depends on the condition of the storage device and the type of data loss.

How can I request a repair?

You can contact us through our contact form. Describe your device and the issue you're experiencing, and we'll get back to you with the next steps.
